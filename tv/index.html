    Help

    No TV answered?

    Type the code the TV shows right now. It changes every time Office TV opens on the TV.

    Same Wi-Fi

    Connect the laptop to the same Wi-Fi as the TV. The picture goes straight from the laptop to the TV.

    Sound on the TV

    Share a Chrome tab with “Also share tab audio” on. The sound plays on the TV only, not on the laptop.

    First time on a Mac?

    macOS may ask you to allow Screen Recording for your browser in System Settings. Allow it, then reopen the browser.
