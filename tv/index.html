    Help

    Where do I find the TV code?

    Open the Office TV app on the TV. The screen shows a 10-character TV code and a QR code. Scan the QR code with a phone and the TV connects automatically.

    The TV is not answering?

    Make sure the TV is on and connected to the internet. Open the Office TV app on the TV once. The date and time on your laptop must be correct. Then press Refresh above.

    My laptop and the TV are on different Wi-Fi networks?

    That is fine. This page sends commands to the TV over the internet, so you do not need an IP address or the same Wi-Fi.

    It says the daily limit was reached?

    Commands travel through the free ntfy.sh relay, which allows a limited number of messages per day for the whole office. Try again later, or open the TV's "Same Wi-Fi page" while on the TV's Wi-Fi.

    Is this safe?

    Every command is encrypted with the TV code. Without the code nobody can control the TV. Share the code only with people in the office. Choosing "New code" in the TV app turns off the old code.
