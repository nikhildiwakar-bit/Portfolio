    Help

    The TV shows as offline?

    Open the Office TV app on the TV once. It stays reachable after that.

    Smooth picture

    Works best when the laptop and TV are on the same Wi-Fi.

    First time on a Mac?

    macOS may ask you to allow Screen Recording for your browser in System Settings. Allow it, then reopen the browser.

    Sound

    To play sound on the TV, share a browser tab and keep “Also share tab audio” on.
